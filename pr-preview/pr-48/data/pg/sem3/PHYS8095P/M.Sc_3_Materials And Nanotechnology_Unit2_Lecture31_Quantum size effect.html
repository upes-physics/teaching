Quantum Confinement

As a semiconductor particle's size decreases below its Exciton Bohr Radius, electrons and holes become spatially confined, much like a particle in a box.

The Brus Equation

E_{gap} = E_{bulk} + \frac{h^2}{8R^2} \left( \frac{1}{m_e^*} + \frac{1}{m_h^*} \right)

This explains the blue-shift in emission spectra as the dot radius (R) gets smaller.
