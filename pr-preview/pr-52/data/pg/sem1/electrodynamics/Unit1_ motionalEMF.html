💡 What is Motional EMF?

Understanding the essence before the equations

Imagine a metal rod moving through an invisible magnetic field. Inside this rod, countless electrons are trapped, free to move within their metallic conductor.

As the rod cuts through magnetic field lines, a Lorentz force pushes these electrons sideways. This charge separation creates an electric field, which manifests as a potential difference across the rod.

Connect the ends, and current flows. Motion has been transformed into electricity.

Motion + Magnetic Field = Electric Potential
