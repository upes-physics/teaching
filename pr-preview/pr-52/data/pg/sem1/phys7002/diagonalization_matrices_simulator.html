Layer 1 — Conceptual Understanding

Diagonalization of Matrices

Diagonalization rewrites a matrix in an eigenvector coordinate system. In ordinary coordinates the matrix may look mixed and coupled; in the eigenbasis it becomes a diagonal matrix that only scales each coordinate independently.

When Diagonalization Works

A matrix is diagonalizable when it has enough independent eigenvectors to form an invertible matrix . Distinct eigenvalues guarantee this for 2 by 2 matrices. A repeated eigenvalue may or may not give enough eigenvectors.

A shear matrix such as has a repeated eigenvalue but only one eigenvector direction, so it cannot be diagonalized.
