Residues, Integral Evaluation, and Cauchy's Integral Theorem

Complex contour integration turns difficult integrals into local information near singularities. Cauchy's theorem says analytic functions integrate to zero around closed contours; the residue theorem says non-analytic points inside the contour contribute exactly times their residues.
