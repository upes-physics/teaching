Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
  • CLO 1: Analyze the 3-level optical pumping transition kinetics ($E_1 \rightarrow E_3 \rightarrow E_2 \rightarrow E_1$).
  • CLO 2: Calculate threshold population inversion ($\Delta N = N_2 - N_1 > 0$) as a function of mirror loss ($R_2$).
  • CLO 3: Evaluate stimulated vs. spontaneous emission probabilities under varying pump power.

Operational Sequence & Laser Physics
1. Optical Pumping ($E_1 \rightarrow E_3$): Flash lamp excitation raises Cr³⁺ ions to broad pump bands $E_3$ using green-blue photons (~550 nm).
2. Rapid Non-Radiative Decay ($E_3 \rightarrow E_2$): Ions rapidly lose energy as lattice vibrations/heat ($S_{32} \sim 10^{-7}\text{ s}$) to enter Metastable State $E_2$.
3. Metastable Accumulation & Spontaneous Emission: $E_2$ has a long lifetime (~3 ms). Spontaneous photons initiate stimulated cascades.
4. Resonant Cavity Oscillation: Photons bounce between High Reflector (100%) and Output Coupler ($R_2$), causing coherent red laser emission (694.3 nm).
