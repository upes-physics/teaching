Forward Bias (V > 0)

The p-side is connected to +ve terminal. Barrier potential reduces, depletion region narrows. When V > Vbi, significant current flows and carriers recombine.

Zero Bias (V = 0)

No external voltage. Diffusion and drift currents balance. Built-in potential ~0.7 V (Si). No net current flows.

Reverse Bias (V < 0)

Barrier increases, depletion region widens. Only tiny leakage (saturation) current ~nA flows. At breakdown, large reverse current flows.
