Theory & Basic Concepts

A Zener Diode operates reliably in the reverse-bias breakdown region. When the reverse voltage across the diode exceeds its breakdown voltage (VZ), it conducts reverse current (IZ) while maintaining a nearly constant voltage drop across its terminals.

Line Regulation

Keeps output voltage steady when supply voltage (VIN) varies while load (RL) is constant.

  • VImin = VZ × (RS + RL) / RL
  • IS = (VIN - VZ) / RS
  • IL = VZ / RL
  • IZ = IS - IL

Load Regulation

Keeps output voltage steady when load resistance (RL) varies while supply (VIN) is constant.

  • RLmin = (VZ × RS) / (VIN - VZ)
  • If RL < RLmin → Diode OFF
  • If RL ≥ RLmin → VO = VZ

Zener Diode Concept Assessment

Answer the following 5 questions. Submitting will display your score and reveal detailed explanations.

1. Under what biasing condition does a Zener diode function as a voltage regulator?

Explanation: A Zener diode is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region. In standard forward bias it acts like a normal diode, but in reverse breakdown, it maintains a constant voltage (VZ) across its terminals.

2. In a "Line Regulation" circuit, which parameter is varied while testing?

Explanation: In Line Regulation, the input supply voltage (VI) is varied to test the regulator's ability to maintain a steady output voltage while RL and RS remain fixed.

3. What happens to the Zener diode if the load resistance RL falls below the minimum required threshold (RLmin)?

Explanation: If RL is too small, the Thevenin voltage across the diode drops below VZ. Consequently, the Zener diode turns OFF (conducts no current), and the circuit behaves like a simple voltage divider where VO < VZ.

4. How are the currents in a regulated Zener voltage circuit mathematically related when the diode is ON?

Explanation: According to Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) at the node above the Zener diode, the total source/series current splits into the Zener branch and the load branch: IS = IZ + IL.

5. What is the expression for minimum turn-on input voltage (VImin) for a Zener line regulator?

Explanation: To reach the breakdown voltage VZ across RL in a voltage divider configuration, the minimum input voltage required is derived from the voltage divider rule: VImin = VZ × (RS + RL) / RL.
