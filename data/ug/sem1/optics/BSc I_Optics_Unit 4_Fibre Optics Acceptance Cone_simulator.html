Optical Waveguide Principles
1. Light Acceptance & Numerical Aperture ($\text{NA}$): Light entering the fiber core within the acceptance cone ($2\theta_{acc}$) undergoes Total Internal Reflection (TIR) at the core-cladding boundary.
2. Mathematical Formulation: $$\text{NA} = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2} = n_0 \sin\theta_{acc}$$ 3. Critical Angle Condition: At the core-cladding interface, light reflects back into the core if the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle: $$\theta_c = \arcsin\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)$$ 4. Normalized Frequency ($V$-Number): Quantifies the number of guided modes in a fiber core of diameter $d$: $$V = \frac{\pi d}{\lambda} \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}$$
