Describing motion

Mechanics begins by describing where an object is, how its position changes, and which reference frame we use to observe it.

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish between distance and displacement.
  • Calculate average and instantaneous velocity.
  • Interpret position–time and velocity–time graphs.
Key idea

A coordinate only has meaning after we choose an origin, axes, and a clock.

Position and displacement

For one-dimensional motion, the position of a particle is represented by x(t). Its displacement between two times is:

Δx = x(t₂) − x(t₁)
