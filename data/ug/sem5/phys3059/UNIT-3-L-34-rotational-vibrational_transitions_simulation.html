Theory

The total energy is the sum of vibrational and rotational energies (Rigid Rotor-Harmonic Oscillator approximation):

E(v, J) = ν̃₀(v + ½) + B·J(J + 1)

Transition Energies (ΔE): For absorption from v''=0 to v'=1 (Fundamental band):

R Branch (ΔJ = +1): J' = J'' + 1

ΔE_R = ν̃₀ + 2B(J'' + 1)

Q Branch (ΔJ = 0): J' = J''

ΔE_Q ≈ ν̃₀ (if B' ≈ B'')

P Branch (ΔJ = -1): J' = J'' - 1

ΔE_P = ν̃₀ - 2B·J''

Note: J'' is the lower state rotational quantum number.
