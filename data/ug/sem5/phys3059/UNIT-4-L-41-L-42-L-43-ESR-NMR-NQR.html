Electron Spin Resonance (ESR)

Theory

ESR (or EPR) studies molecules with unpaired electrons. An external magnetic field (B₀) splits the degenerate spin states (m_s = ±1/2) via the Zeeman effect. The energy difference is ΔE = gμ_B B₀.

Resonance occurs when applied microwave radiation matches this energy gap: hν = gμ_B B₀. The electron absorbs a photon and flips its spin.

ΔE = hν = gμ_B B₀

Applications

  • Detecting free radicals in chemical reactions.
  • Studying transition metal complexes.
  • Dating archaeological materials (bone, tooth enamel).
  • Radiation dosimetry.
