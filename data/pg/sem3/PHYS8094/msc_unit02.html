1) Concept / Theory (read first)

Goal: Use SEMF (Bethe–Weizsäcker / liquid-drop model) to estimate nuclear binding energy and interpret stability trends.

Inputs: Mass number A, proton number Z, neutron number N = A − Z.

SEMF binding energy:

B(A,Z) = avA − asA2/3 − ac Z(Z−1)/A1/3 − aa(A−2Z)2/A + δ(A,Z)

Pairing term δ depends on parity (even-even, odd-odd, odd A). Typical coefficient sets exist; this demo uses a common fitted set and lets you edit coefficients.

How to interpret the terms (what students should say)

Volume term (+avA): strong force saturates → binding ∝ number of nucleons.

Surface term (−asA2/3): surface nucleons have fewer neighbours → reduces binding.

Coulomb term (−ac Z(Z−1)/A1/3): proton repulsion reduces binding; Z(Z−1) avoids “self-repulsion” at Z=1.

Asymmetry term (−aa(A−2Z)2/A): penalizes N≠Z due to Pauli principle / filling.

Pairing term (±δ): even-even nuclei get extra binding; odd-odd are less stable; odd A has ~0 pairing.
